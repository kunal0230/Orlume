Studio Secrets

Techniques From Professional Retouchers

Four practical golden rules to keep your edits looking natural, sharp, and artifact-free.

Preventing Halos in 3D Relighting

Avoid positioning virtual lights too close to extreme depth boundaries (e.g. hair against background). Keep the light distance parameter above 1.5 units and use subtle intensity (15–35%) to create believable studio fill rather than an artificial cutout look.

Skin Tone Protection in HSL Mixer

Human skin tones predominantly occupy the Orange channel (with slight touches of Red and Yellow). When color grading, keep Orange hue within ±8° and saturation within ±12%. Push greens and blues aggressively to achieve cinematic mood without jaundiced faces.

Retaining Fine Detail on Transparent Cutouts

When cutting out subjects against complex backgrounds, do not over-feather the mask. Set feather between 1px and 3px to keep edge contrast crisp, then use a low-opacity eraser brush on the hair boundary to remove residual background spill.

Unlocking Maximum WebGPU Inference Speed

For instant Depth Anything V2 estimation on desktop Chrome, ensure hardware acceleration is toggled on in your browser settings. WebGPU delivers up to 4× faster model execution than WASM fallback, completing full depth estimation in under 350ms.
