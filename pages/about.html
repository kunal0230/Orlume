Hey! I'm an engineering student passionate about computer graphics, shaders, and vision models. I built Orlume out of curiosity to see if a browser can deliver real, 60fps photo editing and AI depth relighting without paid subscriptions, accounts, or uploading private photos to cloud servers. It's completely free, client-side, and open source under the MIT license.

Why I Built This

Most photo tools are locked behind pricey subscriptions or cloud uploads. I wanted a lightweight studio that opens in any tab with zero paywalls, zero accounts, and zero commercial fluff.

Zero Cloud Uploads

There is literally no backend server. Every shader pass, tone curve, and neural model executes directly inside your browser on your device's own GPU and local memory.
