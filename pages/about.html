Sculpting light in 3D directly inside your browser.

Orlume is a personal hobby project built by Kunal Chaugule exploring the intersection of real-time WebGPU compute pipelines, interactive 3D normal lighting, and on-device neural vision.

High-end creative tools shouldn't require monthly subscription fees or upload your personal memories to remote cloud servers. Orlume proves that modern web standards can execute desktop-grade relighting, segmentation, and 8-channel color grading locally on your GPU at 60 FPS.
