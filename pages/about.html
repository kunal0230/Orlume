About the Project

A student hobby project exploring in-browser graphics & AI.

Hey, I'm Kunal. Orlume is a personal open-source project I started to experiment with WebGPU shaders, machine learning models, and photo editing directly inside the browser. No servers, no sign-ups, and no paywalls.

Why I built this

Most modern photo editing tools have either become bloated, locked behind recurring monthly subscriptions, or designed to upload your personal photos to some cloud server where your data gets processed on someone else's machine.

As a student fascinated by computer graphics, shaders, and machine learning, I asked a simple question: What if the browser itself could do all the heavy lifting?

Modern browser APIs like WebGPU, WebGL2, and WebAssembly have evolved dramatically. What started as a small weekend experiment with fragment shaders quickly became a deep dive into GPU rendering pipelines, 3D relighting, neural depth estimation via ONNX Runtime Web, and color grading mathematics. Orlume is the result of that curiosity — a full-featured photo studio that lives entirely on your device.

What drives the project

Privacy by Architecture

Your photos never leave your device because there is literally no backend server collecting or processing them. Everything runs client-side in your browser's local memory.

No Commercial Agenda

No subscriptions, no "pro" paywalls, no tracking cookies, and no ads. This is a passion project built for learning, experimenting, and sharing with anyone who loves editing photos.

Open for Exploration

The entire project is public on GitHub under the MIT license. Whether you want to see how the shaders work, report a glitch, suggest a feature, or fork it, it's open to everyone.
