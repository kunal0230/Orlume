About the project

Why I built Orlume

Hey, I'm Kunal. I'm a student who enjoys experimenting with computer graphics, WebGPU shaders, and browser tech.

I started Orlume as a hobby project because I wanted to see if a normal web browser could handle real photo editing and 3D relighting without asking you to upload personal photos to a remote server or pay monthly subscription fees.

Everything runs on your device's own GPU using WebGPU, WebGL2, and WebAssembly. There are no accounts, no server compute costs, and the entire code is open source under the MIT license.

100% Private

Your photos stay in your browser RAM and GPU. There are no backend storage servers and zero photos are ever uploaded.

Hardware Shaders

Real-time color grading, tone adjustments, and 3D relighting written in custom WGSL and GLSL fragment shaders.

On-Device Models

Monocular depth estimation and portrait segmentation running locally via ONNX Runtime Web and WebAssembly SIMD.

Free & Open Source

MIT licensed. No paywalls, no watermark on exports, and no ads. Free to inspect, fork, or use for your own projects.
